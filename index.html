Trump rejects Iran's 7-day plan to reopen the Strait of Hormuz

Facts

  • The Senate on Friday rejected a war-powers resolution to force an end to the conflict; four Republicans crossed over to vote with Democrats, but most GOP senators stuck with Trump.

Congress avoids a pre-midterm shutdown, funds government to Dec. 11

Facts

  • The House passed a short-term funding bill 370-48 after the Senate approved it overwhelmingly; Trump signed it into law.
  • The bill keeps agencies funded at current levels through December 11, 2026 — just after the midterms.
  • It blocks DHS from shifting funds to Border Patrol and delays a rule giving political appointees more control over federal grant approvals.
  • Rep. Rosa DeLauro called the grants-rule delay "an important first step," not a full fix.

Left view

NPR and PBS NewsHour note centrist Democrats say they had no better option, while progressives are frustrated the deal didn't go further in blocking what they call politicized disaster and grant funding.

Right view

The Washington Examiner reports hard-line House conservatives revolted against the deal over spending levels, arguing it locks in prior spending without real cuts, even as GOP leaders like Appropriations Chair Tom Cole touted the "certainty" it provides.

Watch for

The new deadline lands right after Election Day, meaning this fight resumes almost immediately — with a different-looking Congress depending on midterm results.
