Jensen Huang resigns as Nvidia CEO; Trump says Tim Cook should replace him

Facts

  • Nvidia announced Thursday that Huang has stepped down as president, CEO and director, effective immediately. CFO Colette Kress is interim CEO and keeps her CFO job. The board is searching internal and external candidates, and Harvey C. Jones becomes Nvidia's first board chair.
  • The exit follows reports of Huang's affair with Alice Huang, a former Megaspeed executive U.S. intelligence suspects of working for China, and a second relationship with an Nvidia vice president.
  • Nvidia closed down 4.3% at $166.19 on heavy volume after trading as low as $148.80. Rivals AMD (+5.9%) and Broadcom (+5.5%) rallied.
  • Trump posted on Truth Social that former Apple CEO Tim Cook is the "only" choice. He has no formal say over the pick.
  • House China Committee chair John Moolenaar says talks are underway on a date for Huang to brief lawmakers, with subpoenas possible. Senate Majority Leader Thune says testimony "would make sense."

Sen. Kevin Cramer backs a subpoena. The Washington Examiner frames the story as a China-spy scandal rather than one about the White House. Wedbush's Dan Ives echoed Trump in calling Cook the top candidate.
