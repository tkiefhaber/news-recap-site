Trump waives many Russia sanctions and tariffs under the new Graham Act

Facts

  • Trump signed an executive order Friday waiving many Russia-related sanctions and tariffs under the Lindsey O. Graham Sanctioning Russia and Iran Act of 2026, certifying the waiver is in U.S. national interests (Fox Business).
  • The law, signed last month, would have imposed tariffs of up to 100% on the largest buyers of Russian oil and gas. Trump keeps the power to reinstate them; earlier executive-order sanctions are not affected.
  • Fox Business ties the move to a Trump-Putin deal under which Moscow would eventually release millions of tons of diesel onto global markets, as diesel prices climb amid the Iran and Ukraine wars.
  • Last week Trump said it was "time for Ukraine to get a new president" and warned Kyiv to stop hitting Russian refineries. Graham died suddenly in July.

I did not retrieve a left-outlet piece on the waiver itself. Earlier Ukrainian-press coverage (Ukrainska Pravda) described a bipartisan group of lawmakers urging Trump to use the Graham Act and Congress reminding him of it after the Putin deal, which suggests Democrats and some Republicans will frame the waiver as gutting a sanctions law named for a Ukraine hawk.

Right view

The Fox Business report presents it as a cost-of-living measure: Republicans have pressed Trump to lower fuel prices before the midterms, and the waiver is paired with the diesel deal. No dedicated right-leaning commentary was retrieved, and the article carried no lawmaker reaction.

Watch for

Watch for congressional pushback (the law is bipartisan), Ukraine's response, and whether Russian diesel volumes actually materialize; if they do not, the tariff threat can be revived. Market effect would be modestly bearish for diesel if flows rise.
