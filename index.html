  • Stocks slid hard on Wednesday: Dow -1,153 points (-2.19%) to 51,594, its worst day since April 2025; S&P 500 -1.52% to 7,316; Nasdaq -1.74% to 24,443, entering a correction.
  • Thursday futures rebounded ahead of the June PCE inflation reading and Q2 GDP data.

Left view

Right view

Watch for
