Strait of Hormuz standoff drags on as Iran floats stricter transit terms

  • Iran and Oman say they agreed on coordinates for shipping routes, but Iran's draft plan reportedly seeks to exclude US and Israeli vessels and charge fees, which Washington calls unacceptable.
  • Brent crude sat around $83/barrel; Trump said the war "can't go much longer" and predicted gas prices would fall once it ends.

Left view

Right view

Watch for

Progressive El-Sayed wins Michigan Senate primary, a blow to AIPAC

Facts

  • Dr. Abdul El-Sayed, a former Michigan public-health official, won the Democratic Senate nomination Aug. 4 by roughly a point over Rep. Haley Stevens in the most expensive Senate primary in US history.
  • He overcame about $65M in outside spending, including more than $30M from AIPAC-aligned groups backing Stevens.
  • He faces Republican former Rep. Mike Rogers in November; the race could help decide Senate control.

Left view

NBC News and CNN cast it as a Midwest beachhead for a resurgent progressive left; Al Jazeera frames it as a rebuke of AIPAC's spending in Democratic primaries.

Right view

Conservatives argue a Medicare-for-All, corporate-money-critical nominee is too far left for a swing state and hands Rogers an opening, casting the result as evidence of a Democratic fracture heading into the midterms.

Watch for

Michigan becomes a marquee general-election battleground. Watch whether national Democrats consolidate behind El-Sayed and how AIPAC recalibrates after a high-profile loss.
