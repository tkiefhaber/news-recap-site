Left view

No left-leaning outlet coverage was retrievable today. Environmental advocates generally argue communities should be able to recover damages in state courts.

Right view

No right-leaning outlet coverage was retrievable today. The industry position, reflected in the administration brief, is that climate policy belongs to federal law and Congress, not patchwork state suits.
