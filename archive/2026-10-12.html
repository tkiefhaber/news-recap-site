Facts

  • Iran says the Strait of Hormuz stays closed until the U.S. meets seven conditions rooted in the June Islamabad memorandum; its chief negotiator is parliament speaker Ghalibaf.
  • Iran's earlier offer: reopen Hormuz within seven days in exchange for lifting the U.S. naval blockade, sanctions waivers, about $12bn in frozen assets and a wider ceasefire. Washington wants one package that includes the nuclear file.

Facts
