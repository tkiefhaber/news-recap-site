US–Iran fighting grinds into its tenth straight day around the Strait of Hormuz

Watch for

Facts

  • Burnham was acclaimed unchallenged with 379 Labour MP nominations and pledged to be a "circuit-breaker," promising a 10-year plan to restore stability (CNN, NPR).
  • Trump called Britain a "Poverty Stricken Disaster" as Burnham took over (CNBC).

Left view

The Guardian and NPR present Burnham — a former mayor of Greater Manchester with a more populist, left-of-Starmer profile — as a chance to reset Labour and end years of churn and unpopular U-turns.

Right view

Right-leaning coverage, amplified by the NY Post and Trump's own broadside, frames the rapid turnover of British leaders as evidence of national decline and doubts a new Labour PM can deliver growth.

Watch for

Markets will watch the pound and gilts for reaction, and Washington–London relations after Trump's insult. Burnham's promised economic plan and any early cabinet moves will signal how far he breaks from Starmer.
