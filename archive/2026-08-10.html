Iran issues steep demands before reopening the Strait of Hormuz

Facts

  • Iran's national security chief Mohammad Bagher Zolghadr laid out conditions the US must meet before Tehran fully reopens the Strait of Hormuz: lift the US naval blockade of Iranian ports, withdraw American forces from the region, drop sanctions, permanently end the war, and pay compensation for war damage.
  • Iran says it is close to a deal with Oman on managing the strait, but is holding out for US concessions first.
  • Over the weekend a vessel owned by Abu Dhabi's state oil company ADNOC was struck while transiting the strait; Iran's foreign ministry acknowledged firing on commercial shipping.
  • Trump said the strait would reopen "soon" or he would order a fresh attack, and predicted gas prices would fall once the war ends. Iran confirms messages are passing through intermediaries but declines to call them negotiations.
  • Joint Chiefs Chairman Gen. Dan Caine has privately told administration advisers Washington needs an off-ramp, per CNN reporting.

Fox opinion writers have called any secret deal a "surrender."

Ukraine's drone campaign triggers Russia's worst fuel crisis in years

Facts

  • Ukraine has hit Russian oil refineries with long-range drones almost daily for months, targeting facilities including Ryazan (a main fuel artery to Moscow) and Volgograd.
  • Independent analysts describe the most severe Russian fuel crisis in years; the pace and coordination of strikes leaves Russia unable to repair refineries before the next wave.
  • Gasoline shortages that began in Crimea and the far east have spread to the Volga region and central and southern Russia, per Reuters-cited retailers and traders.
  • The strikes are unsettling the Russian public far from the front lines.

Left view

The Guardian and CNN emphasize the strategic logic: hitting Russia's war economy and energy revenue to pressure Moscow toward the table, while noting the toll on ordinary Russians and the risk of escalation.

Right view

Right-leaning and hawkish commentators frame the campaign as proof Ukraine can impose real costs and argue it validates continued Western support for long-range capabilities; some caution about global oil-price spillover and escalation risk if Russian energy exports are further disrupted.

Watch for

Energy analysts warn sustained refinery damage could tighten global diesel and gasoline supply and add upward pressure on prices already elevated by the Hormuz crisis. Watch for Russian retaliation, export curbs, and any impact on winter fuel supplies inside Russia.

Watch for
