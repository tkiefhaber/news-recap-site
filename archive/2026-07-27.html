Haitian TPS protections end; ICE readies deportation operations

Facts

  • Temporary Protected Status for more than 300,000 Haitians lapses today after a June 25 Supreme Court ruling backing the administration and an appeals-court extension to July 27.
  • CBS News reports ICE is preparing arrests and deportation flights, with early focus on Ohio, Florida, and Indiana.
  • Those losing TPS immediately lose work authorization and become eligible for arrest unless they hold another status.
  • The State Department still warns Americans not to travel to Haiti due to gang violence and instability.

Left view

Time and CBS coverage highlights the human toll — families losing livelihoods and facing return to a country in crisis — and notes many affected have no criminal record. Democratic lawmakers frame it as cruelty toward a vulnerable community.

Right view

RedState and NewsNation frame TPS as a "temporary" status that outlived its purpose, arguing the courts affirmed the administration's authority and that enforcing removal is simply following the law.

Watch for

Expect further legal challenges and possible local-court injunctions. Ohio communities like Springfield could see concentrated enforcement; watch labor-market effects in food processing and logistics where Haitian TPS holders are heavily employed.

Nvidia weighs $250B backstop for OpenAI's Ohio data-center buildout

Facts

  • Nvidia is in talks to guarantee roughly $250 billion in lease and construction financing for OpenAI to use a planned 10-gigawatt data-center campus in Piketon, Ohio.
  • The site — a former uranium enrichment plant — is being developed by a SoftBank energy unit as part of a reported $500 billion buildout.
  • A parallel talk could see Nvidia help fund OpenAI's chip purchases, a figure the WSJ pegs as high as $350 billion.
  • Power supply is separately backed by a $33 billion Japanese investment under a US–Japan trade deal. Talks are ongoing and could change.

Left view

Vox-style and mainstream tech commentary raises concentration and circularity concerns — a chipmaker underwriting its own biggest customer — and flags energy, water, and community impacts of gigawatt-scale campuses.

Right view

Free-market outlets frame the deal as private capital reshoring critical AI infrastructure and reviving a defunct industrial site, and cite the Japan power investment as a trade-policy win.

Watch for

Nvidia dropped ~5% Monday on circular-financing worries, weighing on the Nasdaq. Watch big-tech capex guidance this week (Microsoft, Meta, Apple, Amazon report) for signals on whether the AI spending cycle is peaking or accelerating.
