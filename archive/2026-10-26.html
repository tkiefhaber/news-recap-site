Trump's DOJ moves into the midterm homestretch: Alaska certification suit, raids and indictments

Facts

  • Oct 23: DOJ sued preemptively to block certification of Alaska's Senate result if GOP Sen. Dan Sullivan loses to Democrat Mary Peltola, arguing a second candidate with a similar name could confuse voters. Alaska's Supreme Court previously ruled that candidate belongs on the ballot.
  • Trump said he ordered the suit. DOJ also sued California over ballots from suspected noncitizens, and agents searched Rep. Ilhan Omar's home on Oct 12.
  • Virginia state Sen. Louise Lucas was indicted Oct 22 and two former senior FBI officials were charged Oct 23 with lying to Congress over the Hunter Biden probe (per Axios).

AG Todd Blanche said the Lucas probe began under Biden and that he works for Trump. Trump separately blasted Alaska's ranked-choice voting as "corrupt" (Washington Examiner).

Watch for

Court rulings on the Alaska case before Nov 3 and any post-election certification fights. Control of the Senate (53-47 GOP) could hinge on these races; Democrats need a net four seats.
