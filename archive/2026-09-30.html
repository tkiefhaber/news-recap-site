Trump and AI CEOs sign a voluntary “self-policing” accord at the White House

Facts

  • Executives from OpenAI, Anthropic, Meta, Google, Microsoft, Nvidia and others met Trump at the White House on Tuesday; Axios and NBC report the companies signed a “White House Accord on Superintelligence.”
  • The accord is voluntary and non-binding. Per The Week's summary, it calls for internal monitoring during training and deployment, dedicated safeguard teams, independent external audits, and an independent board committee that reviews audit results; it says it may “make sense to codify” the steps into law later.
  • Trump called it “morally binding” and said companies will police each other. He also ordered federal agencies to say “Super Intelligence” (“SI”) instead of “AI,” per NBC and Axios, and unveiled an AI-built America.gov portal meant to fold in some 29,000 federal sites by 2027.
  • Full text and enforcement details had not been released as of Axios's report.

Consumer confidence sinks to a 12-year low as PCE inflation data lands today

Facts

  • The Conference Board index fell 6.7 points to 81.9 in September, the lowest since 2014 and well below the 89.2 economists expected (Axios, Benzinga).
  • The Expectations Index dropped to 63.6, its third straight decline and below the 80 level that often signals recession risk; 12-month inflation expectations rose to 6.1%, and 68.4% of respondents expect higher rates.
  • Respondents cited prices and fuel costs most; the labor market is holding up, with 46,000 jobs added in August and layoffs low.
  • The Fed hiked 25 bp on Sept 16 in a 12-0 vote to 3.75%–4.00%, its first hike since 2023, and 16 of 19 officials see at least one more this year (Schwab summary). NY Fed's John Williams said there is “no need for urgency,” nudging markets toward an October hold (Yahoo Finance). August PCE inflation is out today at 8:30 ET; core is expected around 3.3% year over year.
